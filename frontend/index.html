What is Waste Segregation?

Most Tier-2/3 households already know segregation is mandatory. What's missing is immediate feedback and a reason to keep doing it - even when the collector quietly accepts mixed waste anyway. Waste Segregation closes that gap digitally, without replacing a single bin or truck already in use.

How it works

Six simple steps bridge the household and the ULB dashboard.

1

Segregate

Household separates wet and dry waste as usual.

2

Scan

Collector scans the household's unique QR tag at the door.

3

Rate

Collector rates today's segregation: Good, Average or Poor.

4

Reward

Green Credits are calculated and awarded automatically.

5

Track

Household views credits, history and redemption options.

6

Analyze

ULB dashboard receives live ward-wise compliance data.

Why it matters

Segregation compliance is invisible to city administrators today. Waste Segregation turns every doorstep pickup into a data point - and every good habit into a small, tangible reward.
